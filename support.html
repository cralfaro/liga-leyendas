About LigaLeyendas

LigaLeyendas is a football competition app for friends. Create or join private leagues, organize games, record results and compete for the top position in the ranking.

The app is designed to make casual football competitions easier to organize and more fun to play.

🔐

Account and login

I can't log in

LigaLeyendas uses Google authentication. Make sure you are signing in with the Google account associated with your LigaLeyendas profile.

I want to delete my account

To request deletion of your LigaLeyendas account and associated personal data, contact us using the email address below.

Please include the email address associated with your account so that we can identify the account to be deleted.

🏆

Leagues and games

I can't join a league

Make sure you have entered the correct invitation code. Invitation codes are provided by the league administrator.

I can't see a game

Make sure you have joined the correct league and that the game belongs to that league. Pull down on the main screen to refresh the latest information.

My result or ranking looks incorrect

League administrators are responsible for recording and updating game results. If a result appears incorrect, contact the league administrator first.

🔔

Notifications

LigaLeyendas can send notifications about important league and game events, such as new players joining a league, players joining or leaving games, and updated game results.

If you are not receiving notifications, check that notifications are enabled for LigaLeyendas in your device settings.

📱

Common issues

  • App appears stuck: Close and reopen LigaLeyendas and try again.
  • Data is not updated: Pull down on the main screen to refresh the data.
  • Notifications are missing: Check your device notification permissions.
  • Invitation doesn't work: Check that the invitation code is correct and has not been changed or invalidated.

Contact support

If you have a problem that is not covered above, please contact us and describe the issue as clearly as possible.

If possible, include:

  • The problem you are experiencing
  • The steps that caused the problem
  • Your device model
  • Your iOS or Android version
  • A screenshot of the problem, if relevant
