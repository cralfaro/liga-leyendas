Common Issues

🔐 Login problems

If you are having trouble signing in with Google, make sure you are using the correct Google account and that you have a working internet connection.

🏆 League problems

If you cannot join or create a league, check your internet connection and make sure that the invitation code or invitation link is correct.

⚽ Game problems

If you experience problems joining a game, viewing a game, or recording a result, please contact support and include a description of the problem.

🔔 Notifications

LigaLeyendas may send notifications about league and game activity. If you are not receiving notifications, check that notifications for LigaLeyendas are enabled in your device settings.

Account Deletion

If you want to delete your LigaLeyendas account and associated account data, please contact us using the support email above. Include the email address associated with your LigaLeyendas account so that we can process your request.
